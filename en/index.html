About Me

From Plant Pathology to Code

As a student, I was originally on a path toward plant pest and disease control. After working in that field for a while, I realized I valued continuous learning and solving concrete problems even more, and increasingly gravitated toward creative, hands-on work — so in 2021-2022, I transitioned into software engineering.

It's been an interesting journey since then: from single-handedly scrambling to keep a customer service platform running, to spending nearly three years contributing to the re-architecture of a large enterprise system — where requirements kept shifting both during development and after launch — and now, on-site at a semiconductor company, learning backend framework upgrades and verification, and helping with deployment process improvements that were completely unfamiliar to me. My work has gradually expanded from feature development to system maintenance, problem analysis, and deployment process improvement.

Each new role, each unfamiliar environment, has pushed me to relearn how to get things done well. That's probably the story most worth telling about these past few years.
